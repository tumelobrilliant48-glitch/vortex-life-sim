# Game roadmap

## Phase 1 — Village life sim MVP

### Core systems
- Character creation
- Movement and camera
- World map with a village
- Hunger, thirst, health, hygiene, happiness, fitness
- NPC AI daily routine
- Money and jobs
- Buy food and basic items
- Vehicle prototype
- Save and load system

### Goal
A small but believable life loop where the player wakes up, works, eats, travels, sleeps, and grows their status.

## Phase 2 — City and economy

### Core systems
- More districts and buildings
- Shops, restaurants, offices, schools, clinics
- Public transport
- Car ownership and modifications
- Reputation and police systems
- Relationships, marriage, family creation
- Home ownership and rent

### Goal
Create an economy that feels real and dynamic.

## Phase 3 — Countries and politics

### Core systems
- Multiple cities/countries
- Jobs and work permits
- National elections and leadership
- Taxes, loans, and land registration
- Government systems and laws

### Goal
Create social complexity and a persistent world that changes over time.

## Phase 4 — Space and planets

### Core systems
- Space travel and rockets
- Planet exploration
- Alien NPCs and worlds
- Resource harvesting and survival suits
- Space stations

### Goal
Expand the simulation beyond Earth and into a multi-world setting.

## Phase 5 — Multiplayer and monetization

### Core systems
- Global accounts
- Secure cloud saves
- Premium content
- Leaderboards and social features
- Marketplace or DLC releases

### Goal
Turn the prototype into a scaleable game platform.

## Development rule

Never build all phases at once. Build the smallest version of the next layer and test it before adding the next.
