# Education and school system

## Goal
Add a school layer so players can progress through education, pass tests, and qualify for better jobs.

## Included features

- `EducationSystem` with course definitions and exam evaluation
- `SchoolMarker` for school interaction points in the world

## Why this matters

This is crucial because it introduces:
- educational progression
- job qualification in the world
- player maturity and life progression
- a stronger simulation loop beyond just survival and money

- family system
- marriage and parenting
- kids growing over time
- relationship simulation
- social and emotional systems
