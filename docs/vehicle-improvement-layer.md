# Vehicle improvement layer

## Goal
Extend the vehicle system beyond a basic controller into a more believable and realistic world element.

## Included features

- `VehicleDamageSystem` for crash damage and repair
- `VehicleLicenseSystem` for driver license checks
- `NPCVehicleTraffic` for moving NPC vehicles along routes

## Why these matter

This is the stage where the world stops feeling like a toy environment and starts feeling like a living world.

- cars can be damaged in crashes
- players must earn or obtain a license
- roads have moving traffic
- the world feels more dynamic and realistic

## Next stage after this

- road network and waypoints
- public bus and taxi system
- car modifications and upgrades
- theft and police chase system
- houses, ownership, and property market
