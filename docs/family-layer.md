# Family and relationship layer

## Goal
Add a social layer to the life sim so the player can form relationships, start a family, and raise children.

## Included features

- `RelationshipSystem` for dating, marriage, and divorce
- `FamilySystem` for child count and family record tracking
- `ChildGrowthSystem` for aging and adulthood progression

## Why this matters

This deepens the simulation into a true life system.

- players can have partners
- players can marry
- players can have children
- family structure influences life progression
- the world starts to feel alive and human

## Next stage after family

- social events
- friend groups and NPC relationships
- city and neighborhood identity
- town expansion
- economy and governance
